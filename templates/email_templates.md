# Email Templates

> **How to use:** Copy-paste subject + body. Fill in [BRACKETS]. Send from your official Weekday email address. Keep the tone warm and human not corporate.

---

## E1 : First Touch Email (send simultaneously with W1)

**Subject:** Interview with [Company Name] - Quick scheduling link inside

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

Hope you're doing well!

I'm [Your Name] from the Weekday team. You recently expressed interest in the [Role] position at [Company Name], and I'm reaching out to help get your interview scheduled.

The team at [Company Name] is excited to connect  here's a link to pick a time that works for you:

👉 [Calendly Link]

It only takes a couple of minutes to book a slot. The company has limited availability this week, so the sooner the better!

Let me know if you have any trouble with the link or if you have questions - I'm here to help.

Best,
[Your Name]
Scheduling Team, Weekday
[Your Phone Number]
```

```
- If anything comes up and you need to reschedule, use the link in your Calendly confirmation email

[Your Name]
Weekday Team
```

---

## Tone guidelines

- Always use the candidate's **first name**, never "Dear Candidate"
- Keep emails under 150 words  long emails don't get read
- One clear call-to-action per email (the Calendly link)
- Never use phrases like "As per my earlier message" or "Kindly revert"  they sound robotic
- If a candidate replies to your email, reply within 30 minutes during work hours
