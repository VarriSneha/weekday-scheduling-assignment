# Call Scripts

> **How to use:** Read these aloud a few times before your first call. The goal is to sound natural, not like you're reading from a script. Keep a notepad nearby to write down what the candidate says.

---

## CS1 - Standard Outreach Call

**When to use:** 2 PM call batch for candidates who haven't responded to WhatsApp or email.

**Before dialing:**
- Have the candidate's name ready
- Have the company name and role ready
- Have the Calendly link on your screen (in case they ask you to send it again)

---

**[Phone rings… candidate picks up]**

You: *"Hi, is this [First Name]?"*

[If yes]: *"Hi [First Name]! This is [Your Name] calling from Weekday  is this a good time to talk for just a minute?"*

[If they say yes or it's fine]:
*"Great! I'm calling because you'd shown interest in the [Role] position at [Company Name], and I just wanted to help get your interview scheduled. I did send a WhatsApp and email earlier with a Calendly link  did you happen to see it?"*

**Possible response A : "Yes, I saw it, I'll do it"**
> *"Perfect! Just a heads up  the slots fill up fast, so if you could book it today that'd be great. I'll keep the link handy on WhatsApp if you need it. Thanks so much, [First Name]  all the best!"*
> 
> [End call. Update sheet: `Call - Will Book`]

**Possible response B : "No, I didn't see it"**
> *"No worries! I'll resend it right now while we're on the call. It's a Calendly link just takes 2 minutes to pick a time that works for you. Can you check your WhatsApp in a second?"*
> 
> [Resend Template W1 while they're on the call]
> 
> *"Got it? Great! Can you try opening it now and picking a time? I can stay on the call if you need help."*
> 
> [If they book while on the call: great, thank them. If they need time: "No problem  take your time and let me know if the link doesn't work!"]
> 
> [End call. Update sheet: `Call - Link Resent`]

**Possible response C : "I'm busy right now"**
> *"Of course, totally understand! When would be a good time for me to follow up? Or if it's easier, I can send the scheduling link again and you can book it whenever you have 2 minutes."*
> 
> [Note their preferred callback time]
> 
> [End call. Update sheet: `Call - Busy, Follow-up at [time]`]

**Possible response D : "I'm not interested anymore"**
> *"Completely fine, [First Name]! Thanks for letting me know  I'll update our records. All the best!"*
> 
> [End call immediately. Update sheet: `Declined - Not Interested`. Notify senior.]

## If the phone rings but no one picks up

**[After 4-5 rings, goes to voicemail]**

Leave **Voicemail VM1** below, then immediately send **Template W4** on WhatsApp.

---

## VM1 - Voicemail Script

**Duration target:** Under 25 seconds. Speak clearly and slightly slower than normal.

> *"Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] calling from Weekday regarding your interview with [Company Name]. I've also sent you a message on WhatsApp with a scheduling link it just takes 2 minutes to pick a time. Please check your messages when you get a chance, or call me back on this number. Thanks, have a great day!"*

---

## CS2 - Callback / Reschedule Call

**When to use:** When a candidate asked you to call back at a specific time.

> *"Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] from Weekday  you mentioned [this morning / at 2 PM] would be a good time to chat about the [Role] interview at [Company Name]. Is now still okay?"*

[If yes - proceed with CS1 from "Great!" onward]

[If they forgot or are busy again]:
> *"No problem at all! Should I send the link again on WhatsApp so you can book it directly whenever you're free?"*

---

## CS3 - Confirmation Call (optional, only if specifically asked by senior)

**When to use:** After a candidate books  senior may sometimes ask you to confirm the slot.

> *"Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] from Weekday. Just calling to confirm your interview with [Company Name] is all set for [Date] at [Time]. Do you have the calendar invite?"*

[If yes]: *"Perfect! All the best for the interview  you've got this! 🙂"*

[If no invite received]:
> *"No worries  I'll send you a fresh confirmation on WhatsApp right now. The details will also be in your email from Calendly. Let me know if you need anything else!"*

## General Call Tips for Ram and Shyam

1. **Smile while you talk**  it genuinely makes your voice sound friendlier (sounds weird, but it works)
2. **Don't read robotically**  pause naturally, like a real conversation
3. **If they sound sleepy or rushed**  acknowledge it: *"I can tell you're busy — this will only take a second!"*
4. **Never argue**  if a candidate is rude, stay calm and end the call politely
5. **No background noise**  find a quiet spot before calling
6. **Call between 10 AM-12 PM and 2 PM-5 PM**  these are peak pickup times in India. Avoid calling during lunch (1-2 PM) and after 7 PM
7. **If you stammer or fumble**  it's okay, just laugh it off and continue. Candidates appreciate authenticity

## What NOT to say on calls

| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "As discussed over mail..." | "I sent you a WhatsApp earlier..." |
| "Kindly revert" | "Let me know whenever you can" |
| "Please do the needful" | "Happy to help with anything!" |
| "We need you to..." | "I wanted to make sure you didn't miss out..." |
| "Why haven't you scheduled yet?" | "Just wanted to make sure the link was working!" |
| "The company is waiting" | "The team is excited to connect with you" |
