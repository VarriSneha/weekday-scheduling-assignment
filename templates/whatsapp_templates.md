# WhatsApp Message Templates

> **How to use:** Copy-paste these. Fill in [BRACKETS] before sending. Do NOT change the core message  these are tested for response rate.

---

## W1 : First Touch (send at 9 AM with every new request)

```
Hi [Candidate First Name] 

This is [Your Name] from Weekday. You'd recently expressed interest in the [Role] position at [Company Name].

The team is excited to connect with you! Here's a quick scheduling link to pick a time that works for you:

🔗 [Calendly Link]

Takes less than 2 minutes to book. Slots are limited this week, so booking early helps!

Let me know if you have any questions. 
```

---

## W2 : Follow-Up 1 (2 hours after W1, if not opened/replied)

```
Hi [First Name], just following up on my earlier message about the [Role] opportunity at [Company Name] 

In case it got buried - here's the scheduling link again:
🔗 [Calendly Link]

```

## Quick Reply Templates (for common questions)

**"What is this interview about?"**
```
Hi [First Name]! This is for the [Role] position at [Company Name] - you'd applied a few days back and they loved your profile. It'll be a quick initial chat, about 20–30 minutes. Here's the link to book a time: [Calendly Link] 
```

**"I'm travelling / out of town"**
```
No problem at all! The Calendly link has slots available over the next 2 weeks, so you can pick whenever works best for you. 🔗 [Calendly Link] -even 2 weeks out is absolutely fine!
```

**"Can we do it over the phone / Google Meet instead?"**
```
The interview format is set by [Company Name], but I'll check with them and get back to you shortly! In the meantime, feel free to grab a slot via the Calendly and I'll note your preference. 🔗 [Calendly Link]
```

**"I'm not interested anymore"**
```
Completely understood, [First Name]! No pressure at all. If you ever change your mind, feel free to reach out. Best of luck with everything! 
```
